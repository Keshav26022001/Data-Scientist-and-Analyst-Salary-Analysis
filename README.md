# Data Scientist & Analyst Salary Analysis
Excel Data Analysis Project
## Objective:
The primary goal of this project is to analyze salary trends and employment patterns in the field of data science and analytics. By examining key factors such as experience, company size, job role, employment type, and geographical location, we aim to provide valuable insights for professionals and organizations.
This analysis will help:
1) Job seekers understand salary expectations based on experience and employment type.
2)Companies optimize compensation structures to attract top talent.
3) Industry leaders identify hiring trends across different countries and company sizes.
## Key Findings:
### 1) Average Salary by Experience:
It has been observed that freshers receive the lowest average salary, while executives earn the highest. This clearly indicates that experience plays a crucial role in determining salaries for data science and analyst positions.
Freshers have an average salary of approximately $100,000 USD, whereas executives earn more than $200,000 USD on average.
### 2) Top countries with companies hiring Data Scientists and Data Analysts: 
The United States has the highest number of companies employing Data Scientists and Analysts compared to all other countries.
### 3) Average Salary by Company Size: 
"Startup companies offer the lowest average salary for Data Scientists and Analysts. However, medium-sized companies provide the highest average salary, even surpassing large corporations.
### 4) Average Salary by Job Role: 
The AI Architect position holds the highest average salary in the field of data science.
### 5) Employment Type: 
The most common employment type is full-time.
### 6) Average Salary by Employment Type: 
Full-time employees receive the highest average salary compared to other employment types, such as freelance, contract, and part-time. This is expected, as they dedicate their full working hours to the company.
### 7) "Job Counts by Type: The most common job type is office work, as companies prefer employees who commit full-time and dedicate themselves to the organization's growth. The least common job type is remote work.
